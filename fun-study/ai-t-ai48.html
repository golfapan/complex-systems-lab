A Project Route: From a Tiny Network to a Tiny Language Model

What deliverables show that the whole learning chain works?

The idea

Build progressively larger but inspectable systems: a hand-calculated neuron, a small classifier, an image or sequence model, and a tiny causal Transformer. Each stage should have a stated objective, data source, baseline, held-out evaluation and a record of failures.

The aim is a reproducible explanation and functioning small system, not imitation of frontier scale. An optional retrieval application can connect the final model to documents. Demonstrations of attention or gradients teach mechanisms, but are not themselves evidence that a language model has been trained successfully.

Read the relationship

correctness → reproducible training → held-out testing → cost and limitation reporting

A model card summarises the task, data, configuration, resources, evaluation and conclusions the evidence does not support.

Work through an example

  1. First verify a neuron's gradient by hand and finite differences.
  2. Next overfit a tiny classifier dataset deliberately, then measure generalisation on a legitimate split.
  3. Finally train or analyse a small causal model, checking tokenisation, masking, next-token loss and generated outputs.

When it applies

A project plan is not a completed experiment. Report actual outputs and measured results separately from proposed extensions.

Check your understanding

What would make a modest project more useful than a large undocumented run?

A clear baseline, repeatable setup, independent tests and honest limits make its results understandable and reusable.
