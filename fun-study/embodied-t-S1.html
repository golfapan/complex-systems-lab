Turn demonstrations into usable data

What exactly is one training sample?

The idea

A demonstration is a time-aligned sequence of observations and executed actions. To learn from it, define observation windows, action targets, timing and episode boundaries. Train/test separation should reflect the generalization claim rather than randomly mixing near-identical frames.

Draw one sample with its sensor times and action times, then state the grouping used for evaluation.

Read the relationship

sample = observation history → future action or action chunk

This is a conceptual or modelling relationship. Its role is to make the task and assumptions explicit.

Try this

  1. Holding out random frames from the same trajectory can leak almost identical scenes into training and testing. Holding out objects or complete episodes tests a different claim.

When it applies

A large dataset can still have narrow task coverage, inconsistent controls or biased failure recording.
