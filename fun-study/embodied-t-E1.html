Build evidence rather than a demonstration

Which observation would show that your explanation is wrong?

The idea

A research claim needs a baseline, controlled changes and evaluation conditions that were not used to choose the method. Ablations ask which component matters. Failure analysis asks where the proposed mechanism stops working.

Write a claim and one experiment that could falsify it. Include success, failure categories and the interaction budget.

Read the relationship

claim → matched baseline → intervention → held-out test → uncertainty

This is a conceptual or modelling relationship. Its role is to make the task and assumptions explicit.

A single successful video is useful qualitative evidence, but it does not establish reliability, novelty or generalization.
