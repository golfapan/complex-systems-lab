Self-Attention, Cross-Attention and Masks

How does one position decide which information to read?

The idea

Attention compares queries with keys, converts the scores into weights and mixes the values. Self-attention obtains these representations from the same sequence; cross-attention reads a different source. The softmax axis determines which candidate positions compete for each query.

A causal mask prevents a position from using future tokens. Masking is part of the information structure, not just a speed trick. Check padding and causal masks separately, and do not treat an attention map as an automatic causal explanation of the final answer.

Read the relationship

Attention(Q,K,V)=softmax(QKᵀ/√dₖ+M)V

dₖ is key width and M a score mask. Rows of weights sum to one over the permitted keys, assuming the row has valid entries.

Work through an example

  1. For scores zero and ln3, softmax weights are 0.25 and 0.75.
  2. With scalar values two and six, the mixed output is 0.25×2+0.75×6=5.
  3. Masking the second key leaves output two, making the permitted information visibly different.

When it applies

Dimensions, scaling, valid mask rows and numerical implementation matter. Cross-attention and self-attention are not interchangeable simply because they share a formula.

Check your understanding

Does a large attention weight prove that a token caused the answer?

No. It is one internal weighting in a larger nonlinear computation; controlled interventions are needed for stronger causal claims.
