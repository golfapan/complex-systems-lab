Model contact and compliance

Why can a geometrically correct motion still jam or push too hard?

The idea

Contact changes the dynamics. A rigid position command may generate excessive force when geometry is uncertain. Impedance or force control introduces a relationship between motion error and interaction force, making the execution strategy part of the task solution.
