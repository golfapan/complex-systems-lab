The Lorenz system

How can a deterministic flow keep switching between two lobes?

Run this experiment →

The idea

Three coupled variables exchange influence through linear and product terms. At the standard parameter setting, trajectories approach a bounded, folded structure while nearby states can separate rapidly. Predicting the exact next switch becomes difficult without making the governing rule random.

Stretching, folding and bounded motion together give a richer explanation than a claim that every exponential separation is chaos.
