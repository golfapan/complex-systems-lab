Detection, Segmentation and Visual Tasks

What changes when the output is more than one image label?

The idea

Image classification assigns a label to a whole image. Detection predicts objects and their boxes; semantic segmentation labels pixels; instance segmentation also separates individual objects of the same class. Each output structure changes the needed annotations and evaluation.

Geometric overlap metrics measure a different question from class accuracy. Detection also needs matching rules and confidence thresholds. Inspect small objects, occlusion, rare classes and missed-object costs rather than allowing one aggregate score to stand for every visual capability.

Read the relationship

IoU=area(A∩B)/area(A∪B)

Intersection over union measures overlap between regions. The union subtracts their intersection once to avoid double counting.

Work through an example

  1. Two predicted/reference boxes each have area 100, with intersection 50.
  2. Their union is 100+100−50=150, giving IoU=1/3.
  3. A detection test must still decide whether that overlap, class and score meet its matching rule.

When it applies

High overlap does not establish correct class, and high image classification accuracy does not establish object localisation. Dataset annotation conventions affect reported scores.

Check your understanding

Can two overlapping people share one mask in instance segmentation?

Not if the goal is to distinguish instances: separate identities are part of the required output.
