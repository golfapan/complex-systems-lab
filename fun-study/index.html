A guide to use, not a syllabus to finish

Start where curiosity takes you. The mathematical foundation pages build reusable tools; the physics pages separate states, trajectories and probabilities; the lab turns simple rules into visible behaviour. The AI and robotics maps show how those tools enter modern models and experiments.

This English edition is written as a self-contained learning resource. Worked examples are deliberately small, simulations are educational models, and reading links point to university material and original research. A successful demonstration is not a general scientific proof.
