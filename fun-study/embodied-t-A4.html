Decide when reinforcement learning is needed

What bottleneck does trial-and-feedback learning actually solve?

The idea

Reinforcement learning optimizes actions using longer-term rewards. It can address decisions not covered well by demonstrations, but requires exploration, a reliable reward and suitable interaction data. Better control or targeted demonstrations may be simpler alternatives.
