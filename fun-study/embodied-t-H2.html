Ask which states are observable

Is a failure caused by missing information or by poor use of available information?

The idea

An image is an observation, not automatically a complete state. Occlusion can hide pose; force can reveal contact that looks ambiguous; proprioception describes the robot itself. History may be needed to distinguish situations that look identical in one frame.

For each task-critical condition, identify a sensor, an inference method or an explicit statement that it remains unobserved.

Read the relationship

observation yₜ=h(stateₜ)+noise

This is a conceptual or modelling relationship. Its role is to make the task and assumptions explicit.

Try this

  1. A gripper can appear closed in a camera view both when it holds a thin object and when it is empty. Force or tactile evidence may distinguish the two.

When it applies

Adding sensors does not automatically solve calibration, synchronization or state-estimation uncertainty.
