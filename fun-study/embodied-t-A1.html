Recognize policy-learning families

Is the policy predicting an action, a sequence, or a distribution?

The idea

Behaviour cloning learns from demonstrations. Action-chunking models predict a sequence of commands. Diffusion and flow policies model conditional action distributions with different generation procedures. These are choices about representation and training, not a simple ranking from small to large.
