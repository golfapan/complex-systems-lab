Close the loop around failure and recovery

How does the robot know that its last action did not work?

The idea

A fixed action sequence can fail after a small disturbance. Closed-loop execution uses observations to estimate task state, decide whether to continue, interrupt, retry or recover, and evaluate the result. Memory can distinguish temporary ambiguity from persistent failure.

Define a failure detector independently of the policy and compare recovery under the same time and retry budget.

Read the relationship

observe → estimate state → choose action → check outcome → update

This is a conceptual or modelling relationship. Its role is to make the task and assumptions explicit.

Try this

  1. A missed grasp should trigger new evidence collection or a changed attempt, rather than replaying the same closing motion indefinitely.

When it applies

Repeated attempts can inflate final success without improving first-attempt reliability. Report both and account for extra sensing or interaction.
