Track latency and execution timing

Can a correct action arrive too late?

The idea

Perception, model inference, communication and control run on different clocks. A predicted action may already be stale when applied. Chunked actions improve throughput but create questions about queues, interruption and how new observations replace old plans.

Draw the timeline from exposure to motor execution, including variation rather than just average inference speed.

Read the relationship

observation age at execution = execution time − sensor time

This is a conceptual or modelling relationship. Its role is to make the task and assumptions explicit.

Try this

  1. At 0.2 metres per second, a 100-millisecond delay corresponds to 2 centimetres of motion, potentially enough to miss a contact event.

When it applies

Faster neural inference alone does not guarantee lower end-to-end delay or better closed-loop performance.
