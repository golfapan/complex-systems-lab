Separate pretraining from physical execution

What does a vision–language–action model add?

The idea

A VLM represents visual and linguistic information. A VLA connects such conditioning to actions. Pretraining can provide useful priors, while adaptation still has to match the robot's observation and action interfaces and the task's data distribution.

Identify which required capability comes from language grounding and which needs sensing, control or new demonstrations.

Read the relationship

pretrained representation + compatible action head + task adaptation

This is a conceptual or modelling relationship. Its role is to make the task and assumptions explicit.

Try this

  1. Recognizing 'open the drawer' does not establish how much force to apply, when contact occurred, or whether the motion succeeded.

When it applies

Fine-tuning, LoRA and direct inference are different interventions. A plausible verbal explanation is not evidence of reliable physical competence.
