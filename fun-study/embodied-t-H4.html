Understand floating-base constraints

What changes when the robot must balance while manipulating?

The idea

A fixed-base arm and a humanoid do not have the same physical problem. A floating base couples arm motion to whole-body momentum and support contacts. The desired hand action must remain compatible with balance and feasible contact forces.

Identify which task condition genuinely requires a floating base rather than merely a human-shaped platform.

Read the relationship

whole-body dynamics + contact constraints + actuation limits

This is a conceptual or modelling relationship. Its role is to make the task and assumptions explicit.

Try this

  1. Pulling a heavy door can shift a humanoid's support demands even if the arm trajectory would be easy for a bolted-down manipulator.

When it applies

An arm-only result cannot establish whole-body balance or recovery. A platform upgrade changes the variables and evidence required.
