Reasoning Models and Test-Time Compute

What does giving a model more time actually change?

The idea

Separate training on reasoning-related data or rewards from spending more computation during a request. At inference, a system may generate a longer continuation, sample several candidates, run a search or apply a verifier. These mechanisms change different parts of the system.

More computation can improve some task success rates, but it also costs time and may amplify errors. Producing a correct candidate and recognising that candidate are distinct challenges. Compare alternatives at matched budgets and inspect whether the verifier is reliable.

Read the relationship

For independent candidates with success probability p: P(at least one success)=1−(1−p)^k

k is the number of attempts. The formula assumes independence and says nothing by itself about selecting the correct answer.

Work through an example

  1. If p=0.2 and k=5, the ideal independent probability of at least one success is 1−0.8⁵≈0.6723.
  2. Real outputs may share the same mistaken assumption and be strongly correlated.
  3. Even when a correct answer appears, an unreliable selector can choose a wrong one instead.

When it applies

Longer visible text does not establish better reasoning, and independent-attempt arithmetic is not a capability guarantee.

Check your understanding

Why distinguish candidate generation from verification?

Because more attempts can increase the availability of a good answer without improving the ability to identify it.
