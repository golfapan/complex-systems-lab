Order, energy and temperature

Why can random flips produce collective alignment at low temperature?

Run this experiment →

The idea

Neighbouring spins have an energy preference for agreement, but thermally motivated updates can accept an energy increase. A Metropolis rule combines this energetic preference with randomness. Temperature controls how strongly uphill changes are suppressed.

A single run can fluctuate or remain in a long-lived domain arrangement. Energy and magnetization provide different diagnostics.

Read the relationship

H=−JΣ⟨i,j⟩sᵢsⱼ Paccept=min(1,exp[−ΔH/T])

sᵢ=±1; temperature is dimensionless here, with the Boltzmann constant absorbed. The proposal mechanism is symmetric.

Try this

  1. Compare low and high temperature. Watch domains and magnetization, allowing time to relax rather than reading the first frame as equilibrium.

When it applies

This finite lattice is a teaching model. It does not by itself locate a thermodynamic-limit critical point or demonstrate a universal scaling law.
