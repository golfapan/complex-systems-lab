Use simulation to test a prediction

What decision would a digital twin help you make?

The idea

A useful simulator reproduces the aspects that matter to a defined question. Visual similarity is insufficient if friction, contact, sensing or latency determine failure. Test whether it preserves meaningful strategy rankings or failure boundaries on held-out conditions.

Choose one paired simulated/physical comparison and specify what agreement would make the model useful.

Read the relationship

simulation value = a prediction that can be checked against physical evidence

This is a conceptual or modelling relationship. Its role is to make the task and assumptions explicit.

Try this

  1. A simulator that ranks two insertion policies in the opposite order from real trials may be unsuitable for selecting between them, even if its images look convincing.

When it applies

Calibration on the same conditions used for evaluation does not establish transfer to new objects or contact regimes.
