Autoregressive Language Models and Generation

How does next-token prediction become a whole response?

The idea

An autoregressive model factorises sequence probability into conditional next-token distributions. During training, many positions can be evaluated in parallel because the full example is known and a causal mask preserves the prediction rule. Generation must extend its own changing prefix step by step.

Greedy choice, temperature, top-k and top-p alter how the next token is selected. These are inference settings rather than new learned weights. Perplexity measures predictive likelihood on specified tokenised data; it is not a direct measure of factual truth or successful reasoning.

Read the relationship

p(x₁,…,xT)=Πₜp(xₜ|x₁,…,xₜ₋₁); perplexity=exp(mean token NLL)

NLL is negative log-likelihood. The tokenizer and the positions included in the average are necessary for a meaningful comparison.

Work through an example

  1. For probabilities 0.25 and 0.75, greedy decoding always picks the second candidate.
  2. Sampling may choose either; changing temperature changes the sampling distribution without changing model parameters.
  3. An inaccurate generated prefix becomes new context, so evaluate complete generated behaviour as well as teacher-forced loss.

When it applies

Align inputs with shifted labels and exclude padding appropriately. Perplexities across different tokenizers or datasets are not automatically comparable.

Check your understanding

Can lower perplexity alone establish that answers cite real facts?

No. Likelihood and factual grounding are distinct evaluation dimensions.
