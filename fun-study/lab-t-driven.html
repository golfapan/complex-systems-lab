A driven, damped pendulum

What happens when a pendulum loses energy but receives regular pushes?

Run this experiment →

The idea

Damping removes energy while periodic forcing supplies it. Their balance can produce a repeating response or more complicated motion. A stroboscopic section samples the state once per driving period, stripping away the obvious periodic timing of the input.

A time trace and a once-per-period return map expose different features of the same motion.

Read the relationship

θ̈=−sinθ−0.5θ̇+A cos(2t/3)

θ is measured from the downward vertical; A controls the driving strength. The drive period is 3π in these units.

Try this

  1. Change driving strength and compare the sampled points. Ask whether they settle into a few locations or continue to fill a more complicated structure.

When it applies

The lab does not perform a full bifurcation continuation or measure a universal period-doubling constant. Finite trajectories and nearby-path separation need cautious interpretation.
